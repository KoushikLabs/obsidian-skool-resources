# InfluenceMap: Mission and Operations

## What It Is

InfluenceMap is a London-headquartered nonprofit think tank (founded 2015) that maintains databases tracking how corporations and industry associations lobby on climate and sustainability policy. Their analysis has been cited in over 2,000 media stories since 2015.

Mission: driving "ambitious action from corporations, financial institutions, and governments" on climate and biodiversity. Theory of change: "meaningful system change begins with measurement and understanding."

## Three Core Platforms

### LobbyMap
"The world's leading database of corporate and industry association lobbying of climate policy around the globe."
- Covers ~1,000 major companies and ~350 industry associations
- Updated weekly
- Scores companies 0–100 on alignment with Paris Agreement (0 = fully opposed, 100 = fully supportive)
- Also tracks indirect engagement via industry associations (200+ key industry groups)

### FinanceMap
"Most comprehensive publicly available metrics and analysis of the asset management sector through a climate lens."
- Examines both investment portfolios and corporate stewardship practices
- Used by institutional investors for ESG integration

### Carbon Majors
Historical production data from 122 of the world's largest oil, gas, coal, and cement producers — quantifying attributable emissions.

## Data Methodology

Assessments use exclusively public data sources:
- Organizational website disclosures and social media
- Top management statements
- Financial disclosures and investor communications
- Regulatory consultation comments
- Reliable media reporting

The Organization Score measures how supportive or obstructive a company's direct climate policy engagement is.

## Regional Platforms

- **Brazil Platform:** 20 companies, 12 industry associations
- **India Platform:** 20 companies, 8 industry associations (real-time)
- **COP Platform:** corporate accountability tools for UN climate negotiations

## Funding & Independence

Funded through philanthropy. Independent of commercial and political interests — critical to credibility when tracking corporate lobbying.

## Key Institutional Relationships

- Data provider to Climate Action 100+ investor process (major investor coalition)
- Referenced by investors, media, policymakers, and campaign organizations globally
- Works with EU, UK, and international regulatory processes

## Why It Matters for AW

InfluenceMap is the closest direct analogue to the "real-time context monitoring" opportunity for animal welfare policy advocacy. Key lessons:
- Weekly update cadence is feasible with their methodology
- Independence is critical — must not be seen as campaign tool of any single org
- Regional platforms show how to adapt to different countries
- Philanthropic funding model is viable for this kind of infrastructure
- The methodology (public data only, scored against objective criteria) is transferable
