# Sentient Futures: About

## Mission

Sentient Futures champions "ambitious builders, thinkers, and doers who refuse to stop at human-centered design." The organization focuses on reducing suffering for all sentient beings through the intersection of AI, emerging technologies, and nonhuman welfare.

Core belief: paradigm shifts require strategic coordination.

## Organizational History

Started as a volunteer initiative in early 2023 when the AI × nonhuman welfare intersection was "largely unexplored territory." Has since grown into an international field-building organization. Registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit.

## Leadership

- **Constance Li** — Founder and Executive Director
- 14-member team including fellows coordinators, conference leads, software engineers
- Board: Bob Fischer and David Pearce

## Programs

### Community Building
- Slack community for professionals in the AI × animal welfare space
- International conferences (SFS London 2026 upcoming)
- Substack newsletter
- LinkedIn presence

### Fellowships
- AI×Animals Fellowship — 8-week remote program preparing participants for careers at AI × animal welfare intersection
- Learner and facilitator tracks

### Direct Research
- **AnimalHarmBench** — first benchmark measuring risk of animal harm in large language model responses
- **AnimalHarmBench 2.0** — evaluates moral reasoning in LLMs regarding animal welfare

### Project Incubator
Research and strategy projects in the AI × animal welfare space.

### SF Residency
In-person residency program for the field.

## Significance

Sentient Futures is the primary field-building organization at the AI × animal welfare intersection. It represents the institutional home of the "TAIA" (Transformative AI × Animals) community that has been developing at the EA Forum. It is directly relevant as:
1. A potential partner/collaborator for AI opportunities in AW
2. A source of talent (fellowship graduates) for AI × AW projects
3. A funder/incubator for new ideas in this space
