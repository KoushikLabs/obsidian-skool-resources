# ACE 2025 Recommended Charities

## Recommended Charities (10 Total)

**New in 2025:**
- Animal Welfare Observatory (Spain)

**Retained from 2024:**
- Aquatic Life Institute, Çiftlik Hayvanlarını Koruma Derneği (Turkey), Dansk Vegetarisk Forening (Denmark/EU), Good Food Fund (China), Shrimp Welfare Project, Sinergia Animal (Latin America + Southeast Asia), Sociedade Vegetariana Brasileira (Brazil), The Humane League (global), Wild Animal Initiative (global)

## Evaluation Criteria & Methodology

ACE assesses organizations based on:
- Theory of change validity
- Organizational health metrics
- Financial responsibility
- Future funding plans and impact potential
- Cost-effectiveness of interventions

The organization notes: "hundreds of billions of animals are trapped in the food industry and killed" annually, framing the scale of the problem.

## Organization Summaries & Evidence

**Animal Welfare Observatory** — drives farmed animal protections in Spain/EU through corporate campaigns and policy advocacy. Launched a multinational campaign targeting Lidl; engaged extensively with policymakers across 10+ meetings.

**Shrimp Welfare Project** — focuses exclusively on farmed shrimp welfare. Secured commitments covering 1.74 billion shrimps per year through electrical stunning deployments; improved welfare conditions of 88 million shrimps via water quality initiatives.

**Sociedade Vegetariana Brasileira** — promotes plant-based eating in Brazil. Implemented Institutional Meat Reduction program in 111 municipal schools; served over 16 million plant-based meals.

**The Humane League** — secures cage-free commitments globally. Working with 129 US companies on accountability; securing 140 new cage-free egg commitments through Open Wing Alliance network.

**Wild Animal Initiative** — funds wild animal welfare research. Awarded $4.7M in research funding to 72 projects; achieved inclusion of wild animal welfare content in academic curricula.

**Aquatic Life Institute** — improves aquatic animal welfare through standards development and policy influence. Being cited in the new Aquatic Stewardship Council Farm Standard Interpretation Manual.

**Çiftlik Hayvanlarını Koruma Derneği (Turkey)** — secured new cage-free egg commitments in 377 locations; helped Migros achieve 45% cage-free egg sales.

**Dansk Vegetarisk Forening (Denmark/EU)** — influences Danish/EU plant-based policy. Influenced the equivalent of $65 million USD in Danish government funding for plant-based development.

**Good Food Fund (China)** — transforms China's food system through chef training and institutional programs. Collaborating with the Culinary Institute of America and Harvard on dietary transition initiatives.

**Sinergia Animal (Latin America + Southeast Asia)** — secured 34 new corporate commitments; expanded Nourishing Tomorrow program to include 37 new commitments, bringing total to 264 locations.

## Impact Metrics

- $58,000,000+ in donations influenced to recommended charities between January 2019 and March 2025
- 167 recipients working on promising projects across 42 countries
- Cost-effectiveness examples: ÇHKD estimated at "three hens per dollar"; ALI's corporate outreach reduces suffering of "thousands of shrimps per dollar spent"
