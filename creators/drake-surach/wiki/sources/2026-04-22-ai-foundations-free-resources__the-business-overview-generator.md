What This Is
The Business Overview Generator is a prompt you paste into Claude that interviews you about your business and produces a structured document you can upload directly to a Claude Project.
Most business owners have never written their business down in a way that AI can actually use. They open Claude, ask a question, and get generic advice because Claude has no context about who they are, what they sell, or where they're trying to go.
This fixes that. In about 10 minutes, Claude walks you through 7 sections of your business. You answer the questions. Claude generates a professional Business Overview document. You upload it to a Claude Project. From that point on, every conversation in that project is grounded in YOUR business, not generic advice.
The document Claude produces follows a specific structure designed to give AI maximum useful context with minimum fluff.
Below is a full guide for using this properly:
Business overview install and use guide
The 7-Section Structure
Claude will interview you and produce a document with these exact sections:
1. Company Identity
What your business does, who you serve, and how you describe it in one sentence. This is the foundation that shapes every answer Claude gives you.
2. Revenue Model
Total monthly revenue, how it breaks down by offer or revenue stream, and whether it's growing, flat, or declining. Claude uses this to give you advice that matches your actual scale.
3. Offer Stack
Every product or service you sell. The name, price, what's included, and who it's for. If you have multiple offers, how they relate to each other. This is what Claude references when you ask about pricing, positioning, or packaging.
4. Team and Operations
Who works with you, what roles they fill, what tools you rely on, and where your biggest operational bottleneck is right now. Claude uses this to understand your constraints.
5. Market Position
What makes you different from competitors. Where you sit on pricing relative to alternatives. Why customers choose you over someone else. This stops Claude from giving you advice that contradicts your positioning.
6. Growth Profile
How customers find you, which channels perform best, what it costs to acquire a customer, and what a customer is worth over their lifetime. Claude uses this to evaluate growth decisions with real numbers instead of guesses.
7. 90-Day Priorities
Your top three goals for the next quarter, the biggest obstacle to each one, and what success looks like. This keeps every conversation pointed toward what actually matters to you right now.

Copy
You are going to help me create a Business Overview document that I can upload to a Claude Project. This document will give Claude deep context about my business so every conversation is specific, relevant, and grounded in real information.
Interview me one section at a time. Ask me one or two questions per section. Wait for my answer before moving on. If I give you a short answer, ask a follow-up to get more detail. If I don't know an exact number, help me estimate.
Cover these 7 sections in order:
1. COMPANY IDENTITY
   - What does your business do and who do you serve?
   - How would you describe your business in one sentence to a stranger?
2. REVENUE MODEL
   - What's your approximate monthly revenue?
   - How does that break down across your different offers or revenue streams?
   - Is revenue growing, flat, or declining over the last 6 months?
3. OFFER STACK
   - Walk me through every product or service you sell.
   - For each one: What's it called? What does it cost? What's included? Who is it for?
   - How do your offers relate to each other? Is there a natural progression customers follow?
4. TEAM AND OPERATIONS
   - Who works with you? Employees, contractors, partners?
   - What are the key tools and systems you run your business on?
   - What's your biggest operational bottleneck right now?
5. MARKET POSITION
   - What makes you different from your closest competitors?
   - Where do you sit on pricing — premium, mid-range, or budget?
   - Why do customers choose you over someone else?
6. GROWTH PROFILE
   - How do new customers find you? List every channel.
   - Which channel brings in the most customers right now?
   - Do you know roughly what it costs to acquire a customer?
   - Do you know what a customer is worth over their lifetime?
7. 90-DAY PRIORITIES
   - What are your top 3 goals for the next 90 days?
   - What's the biggest obstacle standing in the way of each one?
   - What does success look like for each goal?
After I've answered all 7 sections, generate a clean markdown document titled "Business Overview" with all my answers organized under those exact section headers. Use my specific numbers wherever I gave them. Write it in a factual, direct tone — not corporate, not casual. The goal is that someone reading this document could understand my entire business in under 5 minutes.
End the document with a line that says: "Last updated: [today's date]"
Start now. Ask me the first section.
How to Use Your Business Overview
Step 1: Copy the prompt above and paste it into a new Claude conversation.
Step 2: Answer Claude's questions. This takes about 10 minutes. Be specific. Use real numbers. The quality of this document determines the quality of every future conversation in your project.
Step 3: When Claude generates your Business Overview, copy it or download it as a markdown file.
Step 4: Go to claude.ai. Click Projects in the sidebar. Create a new project and name it "Strategy Room" (or whatever fits your use case).
Step 5: Upload your Business Overview file to the project's knowledge section.
Step 6: Add these custom instructions to your project:
> You are a strategic advisor for my business. Use the uploaded Business Overview to ground every answer in real data about my company. Challenge my assumptions. Don't agree with bad ideas just to be agreeable. Reference specific numbers from my Business Overview when giving advice. No generic business advice. Everything should be specific to MY situation.
Step 7: Start a conversation in your project. Ask Claude anything about your business. Notice how every answer references YOUR numbers, YOUR offers, YOUR goals. That's the difference between a generic chatbot and a strategic partner.
Update your Business Overview once a month to keep it current. Your business changes. Your AI context should too.
What to Do Next
1. Run the prompt and generate your Business Overview right now. Not later. Now. It takes 10 minutes and it changes how useful Claude is for your business permanently.
2. Create your first project and upload the document. Ask Claude a real strategic question and see the difference.
3. Keep building. Your Business Overview is the first knowledge file. You can add more: a Competitive Landscape document, a Performance Data spreadsheet, case studies, your brand voice guide. The more context you give Claude, the better it gets.
This is how you stop getting generic AI advice and start getting advice that actually applies to your business.
