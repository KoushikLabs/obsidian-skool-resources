- We communicate to achieve specific objectives.

- Whether you’re facilitating a meeting or giving a presentation, it’s your job to hit the bullseye with your audience.
- Mastering Intention-Based Communication to Collaborate, Execute, and Succeed
- First, what are you trying to achieve? Second, why is it important? Third, who do you need onboard to hit your target? And, finally, how much time will it take? Answering those questions and creating a clear agenda will help keep your conversations goal-oriented.
