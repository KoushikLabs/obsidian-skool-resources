# Elicit: AI-Powered Research Assistant

**Type:** Tool explainer  
**Raw file:** [[raw/Elicit AI Research Tool]]  
**URL:** https://elicit.com / https://miracuves.com/blog/what-is-elicit-and-how-does-it-work/

## Summary

Elicit is the most mature AI-powered systematic review and evidence synthesis tool available. Uses RAG over 138M+ academic papers. Key finding for AW/MEL: it demonstrates that AI evidence synthesis is technically feasible and works at human-level accuracy for academic literature — the gap for AW orgs is corpus (grey literature not covered) not capability.

## Key Points

- **RAG over Semantic Scholar** — grounded synthesis reduces hallucination; citations are verifiable
- **99.4% extraction accuracy** in German Education Policy validation
- **Research Agents (Dec 2025)** — multi-step workflows for "broad topic exploration and research landscape mapping"
- **Team workspaces** — supports collaborative research, not just individual use
- **Built by Ought** — a nonprofit AI safety org; mission-aligned development
