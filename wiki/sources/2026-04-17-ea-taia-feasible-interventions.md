# Animals in AI-Transformed Futures: Feasible TAIA Interventions

**Type:** EA Forum post  
**Author:** Anonymous (EA Forum)  
**Published:** 2024  
**Raw file:** [[raw/EA Forum - Feasible AI x Animals Interventions]]  
**URL:** https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/posts/RM2qfTd3CwykNHsG9/

## Summary

A systematic attempt to list and assess all feasible interventions at the intersection of Transformative AI and animal welfare (TAIA). Maps 15 interventions across a positive-to-negative spectrum. Author's honest conclusion: "nothing emerged as strongly compelling" — uncertainty is high because transformative AI's nature and value trajectories are unknown. Most useful as a map of the space and a set of research questions, not a definitive prioritization.

## Key Points

- **The strongest evidence base belongs to targeted training data** (#10 — CaML's synthetic data approach). This is also the most disconnected from traditional AW advocacy.
- **The most tractable near-term cluster** is working *within* existing AI development processes (#4, #8, #9, #10) rather than redirecting or stopping them.
- **Epistemic and coordination tools** (#1) are assessed positively but with long causal chains — directly relevant to the context monitoring opportunity.
- **AI governance integration** (#8) has at least one documented success (EU AI Code of Practice) and is directly actionable by existing AW orgs.
- **Value lock-in prevention** (#7) may be the highest-stakes intervention but is hardest to assess and most prone to implementation risk.
- The post implicitly frames the near-term window (before transformative AI arrives) as crucial — some interventions only make sense if started now.
