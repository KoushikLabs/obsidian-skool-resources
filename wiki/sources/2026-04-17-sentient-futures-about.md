# Sentient Futures — About

**Type:** Organization overview  
**Raw file:** [[raw/Sentient Futures About]]  
**URL:** https://www.sentientfutures.ai/about

## Summary

Sentient Futures is the primary institutional home of the TAIA field. Field-building org running fellowships, conferences, a project incubator, and direct research (AnimalHarmBench). Founded 2023. Key role: creating the talent pipeline, measurement tools, and community infrastructure for AI × animal welfare work.

## Key Points

- **AnimalHarmBench** is the first benchmark measuring animal harm risk in LLMs — creates the measurement infrastructure needed to evaluate TAIA interventions
- **AI×Animals Fellowship** (8 weeks, remote) = the primary talent pipeline for this space
- **Project incubator** = where new AI × AW projects get launched; worth monitoring for new opportunities
- **14-person team** as of 2025 — substantial for a field-building org
