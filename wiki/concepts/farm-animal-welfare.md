# Farm Animal Welfare

## Definition

The field concerned with improving the lives of animals raised for food — chickens, pigs, cattle, fish, shrimp, and others. Covers the full spectrum from welfare science (measuring suffering and wellbeing) to advocacy (corporate campaigns, policy reform) to on-farm implementation.

## Key Dimensions

- **Welfare science:** Behavioral indicators, physiological measures, Five Freedoms / Five Domains frameworks, sentience research
- **Production systems:** Factory farming (caged, confined), higher-welfare alternatives (cage-free, pasture-raised, enriched environments), slaughter methods
- **Scale:** ~80 billion land animals killed for food annually; aquaculture adds orders of magnitude more fish and invertebrates
- **Intervention types:**
  - Corporate campaigns (cage-free pledges, commitment campaigns)
  - Policy/legislative reform
  - Consumer behavior change
  - Producer/supply chain engagement
  - Reducetarian/plant-based/alternative protein strategies

## MEL Challenges in This Space

*(Koushik's domain — to be developed as sources are ingested)*

- Measuring actual welfare outcomes (not just policy wins) is hard — what changes on-farm?
- Corporate commitment tracking: do pledges get implemented?
- Attribution: which interventions caused welfare improvements?
- Data scarcity in Global South countries where most production occurs
- Long feedback loops (multi-year corporate campaigns)

## Relevance to AI Opportunities

This domain has rich potential for AI applications:
- **Monitoring:** On-farm welfare auditing, computer vision for detecting suffering indicators
- **Campaign tracking:** NLP for monitoring corporate pledge compliance, media monitoring
- **MEL improvements:** Data synthesis, indicator dashboards, automated evidence reviews
- **Research acceleration:** Literature synthesis on welfare interventions, translating research to practitioner guidance
