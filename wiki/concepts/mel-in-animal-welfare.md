# MEL in Animal Welfare

## Definition

Monitoring, Evaluation, and Learning (MEL) as applied in animal welfare organizations and programs. MEL is the practice of systematically tracking progress (monitoring), assessing whether interventions work (evaluation), and building organizational knowledge from evidence (learning). In the AW sector, MEL is underdeveloped relative to the urgency and scale of the problems.

*This is Koushik's core domain of expertise.*

## The Three Components

### Monitoring
Ongoing data collection to track implementation and progress toward outputs/outcomes.

In AW contexts:
- Are corporate commitments being implemented? (e.g., cage-free transitions)
- Are on-farm practices changing? (welfare audits, third-party certification data)
- Is public/policymaker awareness shifting? (media tracking, polling)
- Are animal welfare indicators improving? (mortality rates, injury rates, behavioral measures)

### Evaluation
Systematic assessment of whether a program/intervention achieved its intended outcomes.

In AW contexts:
- Did this corporate campaign lead to real on-farm change?
- Did this consumer education program shift purchasing behavior?
- Which advocacy strategy worked better across different country contexts?
- What was counterfactual impact — would it have happened anyway?

### Learning
Using monitoring and evaluation evidence to improve future work.

In AW contexts:
- Cross-org learning (rare — most orgs work in silos)
- Adapting campaigns based on evidence
- Building sector-wide knowledge on what interventions work

## Known Gaps and Challenges

*(To be expanded as sources are ingested — based on Koushik's expertise)*

- **Outcome vs. output measurement:** Most orgs track outputs (pledges signed, media hits) not outcomes (welfare actually improved for animals)
- **Attribution:** Corporate welfare changes result from many actors' work — who gets credit?
- **Data infrastructure:** Many AW orgs lack basic data systems; data is often held in spreadsheets, not structured for analysis
- **Survey fatigue / access:** Hard to get welfare data from farms directly
- **Capacity:** Small orgs often lack dedicated MEL staff
- **Comparability:** No common welfare metrics across orgs or countries
- **Long feedback loops:** It can take 3–10 years to see if a campaign moved on-farm welfare outcomes

## MEL Across the Organizational Layers

MEL looks different at each layer of the [[wiki/concepts/aw-organization-ecosystem|AW ecosystem]]:

| Layer | What MEL Tracks | Key Challenge |
|-------|----------------|---------------|
| Direct implementing orgs | Corporate pledges implemented? Farm welfare improving? Policy passed? | Attribution, long feedback loops |
| Capacity builders | Did training improve org skills and practice? | Behavior change measurement, follow-up |
| Meta-capacity builders (Mission Quarter) | Did training capacity builders improve their orgs' ability to train? | Very long causal chain |
| Funders / re-granters | Is the portfolio achieving intended welfare outcomes? | Cross-grantee attribution, portfolio aggregation |

Each layer up adds a causal step and makes attribution harder. But each layer up also sees more of the system, creating opportunities for cross-org learning that individual orgs miss.

## Current MEL Practice in the Sector

*(To be developed — notes on what's common, what's rare, who's doing it well)*

- Corporate commitment tracking: Better Food Foundation, Business Benchmark on Farm Animal Welfare (BBFAW)
- Welfare outcome measurement: limited; some academic partners doing formal evaluations
- Cross-org learning platforms: Open Wing Alliance shared learning, some EA-adjacent funders pushing for this

## Context Monitoring as MEL

A specific MEL function that is chronically under-resourced in AW: **contextual monitoring** — tracking the political, media, and corporate environment to inform adaptive strategy. Good adaptive management requires this. Currently done manually and in silos.

AI opportunity: a shared real-time context monitoring platform for orgs in the same country. See [[wiki/opportunities/2026-04-17-realtime-context-monitoring|Real-Time Context Monitoring]].

## AI Application Opportunities in MEL

This is the core intersection this wiki is built to explore:

- **Automated monitoring:** NLP to track corporate pledge compliance from public sources
- **Welfare indicator dashboards:** Aggregating and synthesizing data from multiple sources
- **Evidence synthesis:** AI-assisted systematic reviews to surface what interventions work
- **Survey analysis:** LLMs to analyze open-ended survey responses from field staff or stakeholders
- **Predictive indicators:** Using proxy data to predict welfare outcomes before expensive audits
- **Cross-org learning:** AI-assisted knowledge management to surface lessons across the sector
