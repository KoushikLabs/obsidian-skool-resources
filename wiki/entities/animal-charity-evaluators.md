# Animal Charity Evaluators (ACE)

**Type:** Organization  
**Domain:** AW Ecosystem — Funder-adjacent / Meta (Layer 4-5)  
**Website:** animalcharityevaluators.org

## Summary

The primary independent evaluator of animal welfare charities. ACE conducts comprehensive assessments of AW organizations and publishes recommended charity lists that influence ~$6-12M+ in annual donations. Functions as the "Consumer Reports" of the AW sector — their evaluations shape funding flows significantly.

## What They Do

- Annual charity evaluations (theory of change, org health, cost-effectiveness)
- Recommended charity list + Standout charity list
- Research on AW interventions and cause prioritization
- Influenced Giving tracking ($58M+ since 2019)

## MEL Significance

ACE's evaluation process is the most systematic MEL practice currently applied to AW organizations in the sector. Their framework reveals consistent sector-wide gaps:
- Most orgs track outputs (pledges, meals served) not outcomes (welfare improvement)
- Cost-effectiveness estimates are rough and hard to verify
- Long causal chains (corporate commitment → supply chain → on-farm change) make attribution very hard

ACE is effectively trying to do cross-org MEL for the entire sector — a rare capability, but still limited by the quality of reporting from evaluated orgs.

## AI Opportunity Connection

ACE is a potential early adopter of several opportunities:
- [[wiki/opportunities/2026-04-17-ai-evidence-synthesis-for-aw-mel|Evidence Synthesis]] — they need to synthesize evidence on intervention effectiveness across orgs and studies; this is currently manual
- An AI system that aggregates and standardizes reporting from multiple recommended charities would dramatically improve their evaluation quality
