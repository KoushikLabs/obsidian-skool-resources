# Elicit

**Type:** AI Tool  
**Domain:** AI — Research Synthesis  
**Website:** elicit.com  
**Developer:** Ought (nonprofit)

## Summary

An AI-powered research assistant that automates systematic literature review. Searches 138M+ academic papers via Semantic Scholar, extracts structured data from papers, and synthesizes findings into comparison tables. The most mature, purpose-built tool for evidence synthesis — directly relevant to the evidence synthesis opportunity for AW MEL.

## Key Capabilities

- Natural-language research queries → structured evidence tables
- Data extraction from papers (methods, sample size, outcomes, effect sizes)
- Systematic Review mode (human-level accuracy claimed; 99.4% in one validation)
- Research Agents (as of Dec 2025): complex multi-step research workflows
- Team workspaces and export

## Current AI Engine

Claude Opus 4.5 (as of December 2025 integration) — outperforms Gemini 3 Pro and GPT-5 on data extraction tasks per internal evals.

## Limitations for AW Use

- Searches academic literature only — misses grey literature (org reports, program evaluations, policy documents)
- No out-of-the-box AW-specific corpus
- A custom build or corpus addition would be needed for program reports and advocacy evaluations
