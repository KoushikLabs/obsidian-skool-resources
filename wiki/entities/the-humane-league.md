# The Humane League

**Type:** Organization  
**Domain:** Farm Animal Welfare — Corporate Campaigns (Layer 2)  
**Geographic scope:** Global  
**Website:** thehumaneleague.org

## Summary

One of the largest and most cost-effective animal welfare advocacy organizations globally. Specializes in corporate cage-free egg campaigns. Leads the Open Wing Alliance (OWA) — a global coalition of AW organizations coordinating cage-free campaigns internationally.

## Key Facts

- Working with 129 US companies on cage-free accountability
- Securing 140 new cage-free egg commitments through OWA network
- ACE-recommended charity (multiple consecutive years)
- Operates in multiple countries; significant Global South presence via OWA

## Role in the AW Ecosystem

**Layer 2 (direct implementing):** THL runs corporate engagement campaigns directly targeting food companies. They also coordinate the Open Wing Alliance, which makes them a hybrid Layer 2/3 organization — both implementing campaigns and supporting other orgs.

## MEL Relevance

THL faces the canonical corporate campaign MEL problem:
- They can count pledges secured (output metric) but struggle to verify what percentage of pledges result in actual on-farm welfare change (outcome metric)
- The gap between pledges signed and eggs actually sourced cage-free is significant and hard to track systematically
- Global monitoring (across 129 companies in multiple countries) is a data management challenge that AI could address
