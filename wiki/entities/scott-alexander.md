# Scott Alexander

**Type:** Person  
**Also known as:** Scott Siskind; previously wrote as "Scott Alexander"  
**Role:** Writer, blogger, psychiatrist

## Summary

Blogger behind Slate Star Codex (now Astral Codex Ten). One of the most influential independent voices on AI, rationality, medicine, and culture. Contributed the prose rewrite of AI 2027, turning the technical scenario into a readable narrative. "The fun parts of the story are his and the boring parts are ours" — AI 2027 authors.

## Key Facts

- **Slate Star Codex / Astral Codex Ten:** Highly influential blog; hundreds of thousands of readers; known for long-form analytical posts on AI, psychology, medicine, and policy.
- **AI 2027 role:** Voluntary contributor; rewrote the scenario content for narrative quality. Not one of the forecasters.
- **Rationalist community:** Central figure in the Bay Area rationalist/effective altruism scene, though he maintains an independent perspective.
