# TSMC

**Type:** Organization  
**Full name:** Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company  
**Location:** Taiwan (primary fabs)

## Summary

The world's dominant manufacturer of advanced semiconductor chips, producing >80% of the AI-relevant chips used by US AI labs. Its location in Taiwan makes it a geopolitical chokepoint: Chinese control of Taiwan would effectively give China control over the primary input to the US AI advantage. TSMC is the hardware backbone of the US-China AI race.

## Key Facts

- **Market dominance:** No other fab can manufacture frontier AI chips (NVIDIA H100/B100 series, etc.) at volume.
- **Taiwan risk:** The scenario treats a potential Chinese blockade or invasion of Taiwan as a live option hawks in the CCP discuss as a way to neutralize the US chip advantage.
- **Chip export controls:** US has restricted sale of frontier chips to China; China responds by smuggling Taiwanese chips, buying older generations, and building domestic alternatives (~3 years behind the frontier).
- **Domestic chip efforts:** The US has invested in TSMC US fabs (Arizona) via the CHIPS Act, but capacity is limited vs. Taiwan operations.
