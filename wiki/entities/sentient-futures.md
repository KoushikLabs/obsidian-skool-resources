# Sentient Futures

**Type:** Organization (501c3 nonprofit)  
**Domain:** AI × Animal Welfare (TAIA) — field-building  
**Website:** sentientfutures.ai  
**Founded:** 2023 (volunteer initiative), formalized since  
**Executive Director:** Constance Li

## Summary

The primary institutional home of the TAIA (Transformative AI × Animals) field. Sentient Futures builds the ecosystem of researchers, advocates, and practitioners working at the intersection of AI and nonhuman welfare. Started as a volunteer project when this intersection was "largely unexplored"; now an international field-building organization.

## Programs

| Program | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **AI×Animals Fellowship** | 8-week remote program preparing participants for careers at the AI × AW intersection |
| **Project Incubator** | Research and strategy incubation for new AI × AW projects |
| **Conferences** | International events (SFS London 2026 upcoming) |
| **Slack Community** | Hub for the global TAIA practitioner community |
| **AnimalHarmBench** | First benchmark measuring risk of animal harm in LLM responses |
| **AnimalHarmBench 2.0** | Evaluates moral reasoning in LLMs regarding animal welfare |
| **SF Residency** | In-person residency program |

## Direct Research Work

- **AnimalHarmBench** — first-ever benchmark for measuring animal harm risk in LLM outputs. Critical for measuring progress on targeted training data interventions and AI governance.
- **AnimalHarmBench 2.0** — evaluates whether models reason morally about animal welfare.

## Strategic Role

Sentient Futures sits at a critical node:
1. **Field-building:** Creates the talent pipeline and community for the space
2. **Research:** Produces benchmarks that make AI × AW interventions measurable
3. **Incubation:** Launches specific projects (corporate commitments for AI labs, targeted training data)
4. **Connection:** Bridges AW advocacy community and AI development community

## Relevance to This Wiki

Sentient Futures is the most important organization to monitor for new AI × AW interventions. Any new project they incubate is likely relevant to the wiki's opportunity map. Their fellowship graduates are potential collaborators and practitioners.
