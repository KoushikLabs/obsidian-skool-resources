# InfluenceMap / LobbyMap

**Type:** Organization  
**Domain:** Climate Policy Advocacy Monitoring  
**Website:** influencemap.org / lobbymap.org  
**Founded:** 2015, London

## Summary

A nonprofit think tank that runs the world's leading database of corporate climate policy lobbying. Primary relevance: the operational and methodological template for an AW-equivalent context monitoring system. Demonstrates the model is viable (10 years running, philanthropy-funded, widely cited, investor-integrated).

## Why It Matters for This Wiki

InfluenceMap answers the question: "Can a small independent org actually run a real-time policy context monitoring system sustainably?" The answer is yes. They've done it for climate lobbying since 2015. The AW sector needs an equivalent.
