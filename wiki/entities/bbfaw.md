# BBFAW

**Type:** Organization / Benchmark  
**Domain:** Farm Animal Welfare — Corporate Monitoring  
**Website:** bbfaw.com

## Summary

The world's leading benchmark for corporate farm animal welfare performance. Annual assessment of 150 global food companies on 51 criteria. Running since 2012. Used by 32+ institutional investors for ESG integration. The closest existing analogue to AI-powered corporate welfare monitoring — and the baseline against which any new monitoring tool should be compared.

## Why It Matters for This Wiki

BBFAW represents the current state of the art in corporate welfare monitoring. Understanding it is essential before designing an AI-powered alternative because:
1. It's the existing data infrastructure — any new tool should connect to, not duplicate it
2. Its gaps define the opportunity (150 companies only; annual cadence; no independent verification; no supply chain depth)
3. Its methodology is transferable — the scoring framework could be automated and extended
