# Daniel Kokotajlo

**Type:** Person  
**Role:** AI researcher, forecaster, writer

## Summary

Former OpenAI safety researcher; lead author of AI 2027. TIME100 honoree. Known for a 2021 LessWrong post ("What 2026 Looks Like") that successfully predicted chain-of-thought reasoning, inference scaling, chip export controls, and $100M training runs — more than a year before ChatGPT. Left OpenAI and signed a public letter criticizing the company's safety culture.

## Key Facts

- **2021 predictions held up:** His pre-ChatGPT scenario correctly forecast major trends that seemed speculative at the time.
- **Former OpenAI:** Worked on the alignment/safety side; left under circumstances that drew media attention (NYT coverage).
- **AI 2027 authorship:** Primary driving force behind the scenario; provided the forecasting models and AI research experience. Scott Alexander rewrote for prose quality.
- **Track record matters:** The authors explicitly cite his forecasting success as a reason to take the new scenario seriously.
- **Forecasts:** His median for superhuman coder (SC) arrival was pushed to ~2030 by mid-2025 updates, though 2027 remains a serious possibility.
