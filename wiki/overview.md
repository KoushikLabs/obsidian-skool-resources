## Key Themes

### 1. AI Tools for AW Orgs Are Viable Now — The Gap Is Adoption, Not Capability
Elicit proves AI evidence synthesis works at 99.4% accuracy. InfluenceMap proves real-time policy monitoring is operationally sustainable. Neither requires AGI. The current bottleneck is not AI capability; it's getting AW organizations to adopt tools and building AW-specific corpora (grey literature, program reports, welfare data).

### 2. The Advocacy Asymmetry Is the Strategic Foundation
AI benefits advocacy more than industrial farming because advocacy is information-constrained, farming is biologically constrained. [[wiki/concepts/ai-advocacy-asymmetry|This asymmetry]] is the strategic reason to prioritize AI tools for AW orgs over AI tools for farms. The rebound risk (AI welfare improvements enabling more intensification) is real but doesn't negate the asymmetry.

### 3. TAIA Is the Strategic Frame — But Near-Term Tools Matter Most
The [[wiki/concepts/taia|TAIA]] field maps the longer-term landscape (governance, training data, alignment). The near-term opportunities in this wiki (evidence synthesis, pledge monitoring, context monitoring) are where leverage is available *now*, before transformative AI changes everything. These aren't contradictory — near-term tools build the sector's AI capacity for what comes next.

### 4. The Commitment-Implementation Gap Is the Defining MEL Problem in AW
BBFAW's key finding: 95% of food companies have welfare policies; average score is 18%. This gap — between stated commitment and actual implementation — is the core MEL challenge in the corporate welfare space. It appears at every layer: corp → supply chain, funder → grantee, org → on-farm practice. Any MEL tool that closes this gap creates real value.

### 5. Sentient Futures Is the Key Node to Watch
[[wiki/entities/sentient-futures|Sentient Futures]] is building the infrastructure (fellowships, benchmarks, incubator) for AI × AW work. Any new project they incubate is likely relevant to this wiki. AnimalHarmBench is the emerging measurement standard for TAIA interventions.

## Working Thesis

*The most immediately actionable opportunity is an AI-powered evidence synthesis tool for the AW sector — either built on top of Elicit or as a custom knowledge base of grey literature and program reports. It's technically high-feasibility, addresses the sector's chronic learning deficit, and aligns directly with Koushik's MEL expertise.
